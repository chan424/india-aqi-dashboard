Ongole, October and November 2026

The archive’s case-study table joins three different things on each day: the CPCB city bulletin when one was published, CAMS model concentrations, and ERA5 weather. Ongole’s monitor appears in the 21 November 2026 bulletin (AQI 83). Its coordinates are not in the CPCB PDF or the XKDR directory used for the map.

Bulletin AQI and CAMS particles

PM is a model concentration in µg/m³ drawn on the same axis as the unitless bulletin AQI so the timing can be compared. The scales are not interchangeable.

Ventilation weather (ERA5)

Wind is multiplied by 100 so it is visible beside boundary-layer height in metres. Rain is millimetres.
