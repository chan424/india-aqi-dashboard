India monitor map · Andhra Pradesh & Telangana

Where India measures the air, and where it does not.

Monitors are the CPCB CAAQMS list of 18 November 2026, filled out with the XKDR station directory where that PDF is silent, including the US Embassy sites. Colour is the latest CPCB city bulletin when a station’s city is on it. Numbers tagged Estimated (model) are CAMS via Open-Meteo, bias-corrected and scored with the CPCB sub-index. They are not regulatory AQI.
