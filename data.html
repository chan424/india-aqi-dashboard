Column notes that matter

  • valid_18h is true when a station-day has at least 18 distinct local hours. Charts use those days.
  • value_type in the archive says whether a column is observed, a model, or derived. This site repeats that label on the chart that uses it.
  • CAMS and ERA5 files are models or reanalyses. Bulletin AQI and CAAQMS pollutants are observations.
  • AQLI life-years and APnA shares are models. NFHS figures are survey estimates.
  • The national station GeoJSON is generated for this site. It is listed at the bottom of the table.
  • Census 2011 district geometry is not in the Drive bundle. It is DataMeet’s map, CC BY 4.0, simplified for the gap layer.
